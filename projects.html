Residential Projects

My portfolio focuses on private residential projects where spatial features and interior design are conceived as a cohesive whole. I specialise in new houses, rural buildings, period homes and listed properties, creating spaces that feel calm, natural and enduring. Each project is approached with an emphasis on proportion, material integrity and spatial clarity.

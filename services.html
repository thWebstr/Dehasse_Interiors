Practice Services

Architectural Interior Design

I work with private clients undertaking substantial residential projects, where architectural decisions and interior design must be considered together. Services are typically engaged at an early stage and continue through concept development, detailed design and on site coordination, ensuring clarity and cohesion from structure to finish.

Scope & Focus

Working Approach

Projects are selectively undertaken and typically involve new houses, extensions, full house renovations and sensitive heritage properties, as well as multi room schemes. The work prioritises proportion, material integrity and spatial clarity, creating homes that feel calm, natural and enduring rather than styled or trend led.

Collaboration

Typical Clients

Clients are often commissioning their primary residence and value calm, considered design over novelty. They are engaged in the process, decisive, and understand that quality outcomes require time, collaboration and investment.

Project Typologies

Core Disciplines

Heritage & Period

Listed Properties & Renovations

Thoughtful restorations of historic and listed homes where original fabric, spatial volume, and enduring materials are carefully protected and enhanced.

Architecture & Flow

New Builds & Extensions

Collaborating early with architects to conceive internal spaces, ceiling heights, light wells, and flow simultaneously with exterior structural forms.

Conversions & Volume

Rural Conversions

Transforming agricultural and traditional rural buildings into calm, functional homes with double-height volumes and authentic natural textures.

Every project begins with a clear understanding of how the space needs to function, feel and support daily life.
